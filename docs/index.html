Everything your usage page shows, one click away

Session & weekly limits

Your rolling 5-hour session and 7-day limits, as meters that turn amber at 70% and red at 90%.

Per-model caps

Separate weekly caps, like Fable's, show up automatically as soon as your plan has them.

Pin what matters

Tick any stat to put it in the menu bar. With nothing ticked, Headroom stays a small logo.

Five display styles

Plain text, colored text, mini meters, meters with percentages, or speedometer-style dials.

Reset countdowns

Know exactly when each limit resets. If a refresh fails, the last numbers stay up with a warning.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no servers. It only talks to api.anthropic.com, using your own login.

Pick a menu bar style

Choose how pinned stats look: Text, Color, Meter, Meter + % or Dial. Every style adapts to light and dark menu bars.

How it works

  1. Download the DMG, drag Headroom to Applications and open it.
  2. Sign in: if you use Claude Code, Headroom picks up its login from your Keychain. Otherwise, paste an OAuth token under Account.
  3. Pin the limits you care about and choose a style. Headroom refreshes every minute.

FAQ

Is Headroom made by Anthropic?

No. Headroom is an independent, open-source app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude and Anthropic are trademarks of Anthropic, PBC.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the same usage data that claude.ai (Settings → Usage) and Claude Code's /usage command show, read with your own login. Anthropic hasn't documented this endpoint, so it may change.

Do I need an API key?

No. If you use Claude Code, Headroom reads its saved login from the macOS Keychain; macOS asks your permission once. You can also paste an OAuth token, which Headroom stores in your Keychain.

Does Headroom collect any data?

No. There are no analytics and no servers. The only network requests go to api.anthropic.com to read your usage.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

Is it free?

Yes. Headroom is free and open source under the MIT license. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.
